# Generative-AI assistance declaration

OpenAI Codex was used as a software-development and research-documentation
assistant during this thesis. Its assistance included code drafting and review,
test construction, repository maintenance, literature-record organization,
table/figure preparation and language editing. The language models evaluated by
the thesis are also research objects and are identified separately in the
Methods and experimental-condition descriptions.

The researcher selected the research scope, supplied and authorized the data,
made or explicitly delegated recorded design decisions, reviewed the human
evaluation process, and remains responsible for every claim, citation, result
and submitted sentence. AI-generated suggestions were not treated as evidence;
numeric claims were tied to repository artifacts and literature claims to cited
sources. OpenAI Codex is not an author.

This declaration should be adapted only to the exact wording/location required
by the university or publication venue; its substantive disclosure must not be
removed.
