**Subject:** Request for ethics determination: minimal-risk BSc thesis human evaluation

Dear `[Supervisor/Chair/Ethics Committee]`,

I am conducting the BSc thesis *A Neuro-Symbolic Multi-Agent Framework for
Pre-Release Audience Response Generation in Bangla Cinema: A
Verifier-in-the-Loop Approach* at `[department and university]` under
`[supervisor]`.

I request a written institutional determination for a minimal-risk human
evaluation component conducted on 23 August 2026:

- Three adult (18+) native-Bangla university batchmates independently classify
  100 short generated Bangla film comments into one of two predefined
  engagement-specificity levels.
- Participants see a film-plot synopsis, one generated comment, the two level
  definitions and a forced binary response. They are not asked for sensitive or
  personal information.
- Participation is voluntary. Written informed consent was obtained before
  annotation, including permission to use anonymized responses in the thesis,
  academic publications and research artifacts.
- No monetary honorarium is paid; refreshments are provided.
- Names and consent evidence remain private and outside the research repository.
  Committed response data uses only codes A/B/C. No demographic attributes
  beyond adult status and native-Bangla eligibility are collected.
- Participants may withdraw before anonymized aggregation. No academic outcome
  or relationship is affected by participation or withdrawal.
- The task involves ordinary screen reading; no risk beyond minor time/reading
  burden is anticipated.

3. The institution has no applicable research-ethics review mechanism for this
   BSc activity.

If prior approval should have been obtained, please advise the appropriate
transparent corrective action. I will not describe the study as approved or
