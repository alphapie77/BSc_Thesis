Nowadays, sentiment analysis of textual data has developed into an indispensable instrument
for determining the public’s views and forecasting the results in the cinema industry. Automated opinion mining is a method that allows researchers and the film industry to get the
signals of understanding coming from large numbers of movie reviews and social media interactions that are increasingly powerful in deciding the consumers’ choice. For example, studies that combine aspect-based sentiment analysis with econometric modeling have provided
evidence that the content of textual reviews, not just ratings, has a significant impact on box
office revenues and viewer choices, thereby indicating the use of sentiment analytics as a potentially powerful tool for predicting commercial success [1]. In addition, studies concerned
with box office forecasting declared that the use of sentiment scores derived from movie
review texts combined with predictive models sharpened the forecasting accuracy, attaining
better results than traditional quantitative indicators used alone. The findings have been such
that the forecasting models with sentiment infusion have been proven to be superior to the
baseline econometric models in predicting both the opening-week and long-term revenue
trends [2]. Not just in predictive analytics, but also in deep learning-based models, which
include convolutional, recurrent, and attention-based neural network models, advances have
been made to model the sentiment pattern of movie reviews through deep learning techniques, thus providing better classification performance and comprehension of context than
traditional machine learning methods. Such deep learning methods are able to recognize the
nuances in syntax and semantics of the text feed more expertly, hence providing a superior
representation of emotions for further analytics tasks [3]. This constraint is especially severe
in the case of low-resource languages like Bengali, where the lack of labeled persona data
and the rich linguistic diversity that complicates automated analysis are both factors that
contribute to the problem. Research done in parallel on Retrieval-Augmented Generation
