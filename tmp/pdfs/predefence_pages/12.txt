1.3 Problem Statement
The film industry in Bangladesh has no automatic and systematic method for audience analysis before release; hence, the producers are left with their intuition, expensive focus groups,
or feedback from the audience after the release as their only options. The existing AI-backed
sentiment analysis techniques categorize the viewers into one group and do not present
persona-specific conduct, especially in the case of Bangla, which is a low-resourced language
with very little high-quality labeled data. Besides, the upsurge in the adoption of foreign film
influences has led to a decrease in the cultural affiliation with the local viewers, while the
current neural models are working as black boxes with very little explainability, and the validation of the generated responses is not reliable. Consequently, the need for an integrated,
production-ready AI framework that can simulate, validate, and explain persona-aware audience reactions specifically to the Bangladeshi cinema ecosystem is of utmost importance.
The research questions focusing on these problems are as follows:
1. What methods could be employed to map out the audience personas for Bangladeshi
cinema using supervision techniques on vast amounts of unlabeled Bangla movie reviews?
2. How far can a neuro-symbolic multi-agent system go in producing reviews of Bengali
movies that are persona-aware and culturally aligned for pre-release audience simulation in a very similar manner?
3. What is the impact of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) on the creation of
persona-specific Bengali texts in terms of concealment of the context and loss of linguistic authenticity?
