1.4 Aims & Objectives
The main goal of this study is to create an automated, persona-aware, and explainable AI
framework capable of simulating the pre-release audience responses to films from Bangladesh.
With the use of actual Bangla-language audience data, the research intends to facilitate culturally compatible filmmaking and promote decision-making based on data before the finalization of the script and the commencement of the shooting. The particular objectives of this
research are as follows:
• To identify distinct Bangladeshi audience personas hidden in vast arrays of unlabeled
Bangla movie reviews by combining weak supervision and clustering techniques.
• To design and implement a neuro-symbolic multi-agent system capable of producing
culturally relevant and persona-tailored Bengali movie reviews for simulating the audience reception of pre-release films.
• To integrate Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and hybrid neural-symbolic validation to enhance contextual relevance, maintain steady persona consistency, and
improve the reliability of generated audience responses.
• To evaluate the proposed framework’s effectiveness through quantifiable metrics, comparison with baselines, and explainability analysis, thereby demonstrating its relevance to decision-making in the Bangladeshi film industry.
