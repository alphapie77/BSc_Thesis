1.6 Research Contributions
This paper presents the following key contributions to the fields of Natural Language Processing and Computational Social Science:
• Neuro-Symbolic Multi-Agent Framework: We introduce the first comprehensive
system capable of generating persona-based text outputs, specifically tailored for the
low-resource Bangla language.
• Weak Supervision for Persona Discovery: The proposed framework utilizes an automated process to detect essential audience groups, effectively eliminating the need
for manual human annotation.
• Hybrid Neural-Symbolic Validation: We developed a validation mechanism that
combines neural scoring methods with symbolic rule systems to achieve high-precision
persona matching.
• RAG Integration: The integration of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) allows
the system to retrieve specific persona examples, significantly improving the contextual accuracy and linguistic authenticity of the generated text.
• Production-Ready Pipeline: The research offers a complete, operational pipeline
designed for deployment in actual business environments for real-world applications.
