1.7 Thesis Organization
The rest of the thesis is organized as follows:
• Chapter 1: Introduction – outlines the research motivation, problem statement, and
objectives. It introduces the neuro-symbolic multi-agent framework as a solution to
the lack of automated audience analysis tools.
• Chapter 2: Literature Review – surveys existing works on sentiment analysis, persona modeling, and Bangla NLP. It highlights the research gap and positions the proposed work within the domain of neuro-symbolic AI.
• Chapter 3: Methodology – describes the system architecture, including data collection, weak supervision for persona discovery, and the implementation of the multiagent framework.
• Chapter 4: Experiments and Results – presents the quantitative and qualitative analysis of the system, comparing its performance against baseline models in terms of
accuracy and persona consistency.
• Chapter 5: Conclusion and Future Work – concludes the thesis with a summary of
contributions and outlines potential directions for future research.
