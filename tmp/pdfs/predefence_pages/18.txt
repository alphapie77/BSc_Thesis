2.4 Bangla Natural Language Processing
Significant contributions have been made in the domain of Bangla NLP. Hasan et al. [29]
presented a large manually annotated dataset of 33,606 Bangla news tweets and comments.
Their study demonstrated that monolingual transformer-based models delivered superior performance compared to larger models like GPT-4 in zero-shot settings.
Focusing on sentiment analysis, Islam et al. [30] introduced BanglaASTE, a novel framework for Aspect Sentiment Triplet Extraction. Their work included the creation of the first
annotated Bangla ASTE dataset with 3,345 reviews and the development of a hybrid classification framework combining BanglaBERT contextual embeddings with XGBoost algorithms.
