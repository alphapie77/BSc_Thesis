engineering and weak supervision with multi-agent retrieval-augmented generation and hybrid validation and explanation methods and all evaluation processes.

The methodology begins with Phase 1: Data Engineering which requires the collection
of a large-scale Bangla movie review dataset that contains approximately 10,000 samples
from social media platforms and online movie discussion forums. LaBSE sentence embeddings are generated and clustered through K-means to discover latent audience personas
which do not require manual annotation. The process enables weakly supervised persona
discovery which establishes the base for modeling with persona-aware systems. Phase 2:
Validator Training works to create a dependable persona classification system through the
training and evaluation of various transformer-based models. The research team uses crossvalidation to test the model’s ability to perform well across different scenarios. The bestperforming model becomes the neural component of the persona validator which later serves
