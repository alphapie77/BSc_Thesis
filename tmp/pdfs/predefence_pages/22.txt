and generate reviews in real time for their practical work. The system uses Explainable AI
methods which include LIME and SHAP and attention visualization and decision tracing to
achieve its explainability goals in Phase 5: Explainability. The phase reveals how persona
classification works and how generation processes operate because it removes the black-box
restriction that exists in neural-only systems. The system evaluation in Phase 6: Evaluation

quantitative robustness through this process.
