Regex-Based Text Cleaning:
The process involves using regular expressions to execute text cleaning operations which
remove all non-informative elements and noisy components that frequently occur in social
media data. The process involved deleting all URL links and email address data and HTML
tag elements and special entities and user mentions and hashtag symbols. The team standardized excessive whitespace while they removed all leading and trailing spaces. The step
eliminated non-linguistic artifacts which would have hindered the analysis, but it maintained
the Bangla text’s essential semantic meaning.
Word Count Calculation Using Bengali-Aware Tokenization:
The team used Bengali-aware regular expression patterns, which detect Bangla Unicode characters, to calculate word counts needed for accurate textual length assessment. The approach
enabled researchers to correctly manage the morphology of the Bangla language while maintaining accurate results, which originated from tokenization methods based on English. The
team used word count numbers to conduct both data quality assessment and review filtering
processes.
Removal of Short Reviews:
The lack of sufficient semantic content in extremely short reviews leads to negative effects on
multiple downstream processes, including persona modeling and retrieval tasks. The dataset
lost all reviews which contained fewer than three words. The filtering process removed nonessential samples from the dataset, which enabled us to keep authentic views of the audience
material.
Duplicate Review Removal:
The team used cleaned text material to locate duplicate reviews, which they then removed
from the dataset to stop redundant content and biasing effects. The team kept only the first
version of each distinct review. The process cut down on duplicated content, which came
from users who copied and shared material, while it brought in new content to the dataset.
