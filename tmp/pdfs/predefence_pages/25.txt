Removing Stopwords:
Commonly used words that have little importance in sentiment analysis, such as articles
(a, an, the), prepositions (on, in, at), and conjunctions (and, but, or), are removed. Stop
word removal helps to diminish data noise. In the same way, in Bangla, the terms "অতএব",
"অথচ", "অথবা", "অনুযায়ী", "এটা", "এটাই" etc. are also recognized as stopwords. Table 3.3
shows examples of stopword removal.

Stemming:
The word stemming is used to denote the process of reducing different forms of a word to
its root. Depending on the usage, a word can have different forms. To illustrate, "করা",
"করিছ", "করিছলাম", "করিছেল", "কেরেছ", "কেরিছ" etc. are different forms of the same root
"কর". Stemming is primarily intended to combine different conjugational forms of a word
into one basic form.
