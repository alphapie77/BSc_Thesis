Feature Extraction Technique (TF-IDF):
TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) is an estimation in numbers that
indicates a word’s significance in a particular text concerning a total of documents (corpus).
TF-IDF is the result of the synthesis of two parts:
Term Frequency (TF): The primary aspect of this component is to find the number of
times a certain term occurs in the particular document. The method used to obtain this
number is the ratio of the number of occurrences of that term in the document to the total
number of terms in the document.
