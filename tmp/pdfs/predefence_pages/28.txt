3.3.4 Cluster-wise Sentiment Estimation
The researchers used a lightweight pattern-based sentiment estimation method to create cluster descriptions. The average sentiment scores for each cluster were calculated, producing a
clear measure of emotional intensity. The results showed that Cluster 2 exhibited the highest
average sentiment, while Clusters 0 and 1 demonstrated relatively lower sentiment intensity.
This analysis enabled cluster differentiation through both semantic similarities and varying
levels of emotional engagement.
3.3.5 Persona Assignment and Interpretation
The researchers created audience personas corresponding to each cluster based on its distribution pattern and average sentiment strength. Three distinct personas were identified:
• Indifferent Casual: Low engagement and weak emotional response
• Enthusiastic Casual: Moderate engagement with balanced sentiment
• Die-hard Fan: High engagement with strong positive sentiment
