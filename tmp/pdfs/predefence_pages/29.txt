3.3.6 Dataset Finalization
The final output of Phase 1 is a complete dataset containing 6,114 Bengali movie reviews that
have been processed and assigned specific persona labels. Each entry includes the cleaned
text along with its semantic embeddings, cluster assignments, sentiment estimates, and corresponding persona label.
The dataset was stored as step2_clustered_data.csv and serves as the primary input for
validator training and multi-agent simulation in the subsequent phases.
3.3.7 Phase 1 Outcome
This phase successfully transforms raw and noisy Bengali movie reviews into a structured
dataset containing semantic information and identity-based attributes. The proposed method
establishes a scalable solution that requires minimal manual annotation to generate audience
simulations for low-resource language environments. By leveraging unsupervised clustering and weak sentiment signals, the framework ensures efficient persona construction while
preserving meaningful semantic representation of the data.
