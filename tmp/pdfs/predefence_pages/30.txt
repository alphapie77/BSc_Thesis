3.4 PHASE 2: Validator Training
The second phase of the proposed framework focuses on training a robust persona validation model which can accurately classify Bengali movie reviews into predefined audience
personas. The research demonstrates that transformer-based language models show high
effectiveness for low-resource language classification tasks which include Bengali text analysis. The phase establishes that unsupervised clustering results in valid persona assignments
which possess both reliability and generalization capabilities.
3.4.1 Dataset Splitting
The complete persona-labeled dataset, containing 6,114 Bengali movie reviews, was divided
into training and testing subsets using an 80:20 distribution. A total of 4,891 samples were
used for training, while 1,223 samples were reserved for evaluation. This split strategy is
commonly used in supervised text classification tasks, as it facilitates effective model learning while ensuring reliable performance assessment [28].
