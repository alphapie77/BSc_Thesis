3.4.3 Persona Classification Model Training
Two BanglaBERT-based classification models were trained to evaluate the impact of different hyperparameter configurations on model performance. Prior studies indicate that variations in learning rates and training epochs during transformer fine-tuning can significantly
influence classification outcomes [34, 35].
1. BanglaBERT (Standard Configuration): The model was trained with a learning
rate of 2 × 10−5 for four epochs. Early stopping was applied, and convergence was
achieved after two epochs. The model obtained an accuracy of 85.04% and an F1-
score of 0.8445.
2. BanglaBERT (Advanced Configuration): This model used a learning rate of 2.5 ×
10−5 and was trained for six full epochs without early stopping. It achieved the best performance, reaching 87.49% accuracy and an F1-score of 0.8733. Figure 3.7 illustrates
the details of the training dynamics. Based on prior findings that extended fine-tuning
can enhance transformer performance [34], This model (Advanced configured model)
was selected as the primary validation model for subsequent stages.
3.4.4 Cross-Validation Analysis
The research team conducted a 5-fold cross-validation to evaluate the model’s performance
using a logistic regression classifier applied to the extracted feature data. Cross-validation
is a standard statistical method for assessing model stability while mitigating the risk of
overfitting [36]. The analysis produced an F1-score of 0.9750 with a standard deviation of
±0.0046, indicating that the model maintained consistent performance and generalizability
across unseen data. The detailed performance metrics are illustrated in Figure 3.6.
