Chapter 4
Experiments and Evaluation
This chapter presents the detailed experiments and results of the Phase 3: Multi-Agent
System, which demonstrates its capability to produce Bengali reviews that match specific
user personas. The system demonstrates its effectiveness and reliability through performance
metrics, hybrid validation, and self-correction mechanisms.
4.1 Dataset Description
The dataset consists of 9,998 Bengali movie reviews collected from multiple online platforms, reflecting diverse audience opinions and engagement levels. The final cleaned dataset
contains 6,114 unique reviews after pre-processing removed all short reviews and duplicated
entries.
