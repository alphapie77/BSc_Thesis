The reasoning process involves multiple specialized agents that collaboratively retrieve contextual information, generate persona-aware responses, and evaluate outputs through an iterative self-correction mechanism. This design enables structured reasoning while maintaining
linguistic fluency. For reranking and validation, the system applies a hybrid neural-symbolic
strategy. The primary neural validator is a fine-tuned BanglaBERT classifier that evaluates
persona alignment and produces confidence scores. The neural confidence score is then
combined with a symbolic scorer that analyzes linguistic features, stylistic patterns, and
emotional indicators associated with specific personas.
The final reranking decision is computed using a weighted scoring function:

This weighted combination enhances system reliability, interpretability, and robustness,
while achieving improved performance compared to purely neural reranking approaches. By
integrating neural reasoning with symbolic reranking mechanisms, the proposed framework
effectively generates persona-consistent content in low-resource languages. At the same
time, it maintains transparency, explainability, and deployment readiness.
