4.3 Results and Analysis
4.3.1 Dataset & Personas:
The researchers cleaned 6,114 Bengali reviews, representing 612 of the total 9,998 reviews.
Three distinct personas were identified:
• Indifferent Casual: 2,773 reviews (45.4%)
• Enthusiastic Casual: 1,964 reviews (32.1%)
• Die-hard Fan: 1,377 reviews (22.5%)
User engagement was measured through sentiment scores, which increased from 2.87 to
3.46.
4.3.2 Validator Performance (BanglaBERT):
• Accuracy: 87.49%, F1-Score: 0.8733
• Fivefold cross-validation F1-score: 97.5% with 𝑝 < 0.05
• Capable of identifying minor differences between distinct user personas
4.3.3 Multi-Agent System Results:
• The four-agent system included: Researcher, Writer, Critic, and Reflector
• Achieved (90+)% accuracy in matching users with their respective personas
• Successfully produced reviews on the first attempt without retries
• Hybrid validation (neural + linguistic) effectively resolves persona identity challenges
• Self-correcting system generates reliable and dependable results
4.3.4 Key Outcome:
• Successfully produced Bengali reviews that incorporated persona information
• Provides efficient output that is explainable and usable in practical scenarios
• Established a framework for upcoming tasks, including multi-agent simulation, evaluation, and explanation processes
