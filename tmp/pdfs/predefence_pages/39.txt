Chapter 5
Conclusion and Future Direction
5.1 Conclusion
The thesis proposes a Neuro-Symbolic Multi-Agent Framework which simulates pre release audience testing for Bangla films while solving two major challenges linked to lowresource NLP systems and persona-based text creation. Here, weak supervision was used to
process 6,114 Bengali movie reviews which resulted in discovering three different audience
personas without needing any manual work. The BanglaBERT validator achieved 87.49%
accuracy through fine-tuning, which established a dependable validation system.
The system combines RAG-based context retrieval with a self-correcting multi-agent architecture, which uses a hybrid neural-symbolic validation mechanism to assess neural predictions and linguistic features. The approach reached 100% persona accuracy because it
needed only one generation attempt, which proved its reliability and effective performance.
The framework provides explainability together with production readiness and generalizable
capabilities while it delivers academic neuro-symbolic AI contributions and practical benefits to the Bengali film industry.
5.2 Future Direction
The project reached its first three phases, which included Data Engineering, Validator
Training, and Neuro-Symbolic Multi-Agent System development. The upcoming phases
will concentrate on improving the system, conducting assessments, and deploying the system
in actual operational environments.
