Abstract
The accurate foreshadowing of the audience’s responses and reactions to a film even before
its release continues to present a major challenge in the Bengali film industry; very often,
the result is wrong marketing campaigns, and ultimately, the financial risk goes up. The
majority of the current sentiment analysis methods are reliant on data collected after the release and first getting the general character of the audience’s reactions, therefore missing out
on behavior that is specific to each persona. These drawbacks are made even worse in the
case of low-resource languages like Bengali, where it is hard to find labeled datasets, and
the language is extremely context-dependent. The dissertation introduces a neuro-symbolic
multi-agent framework that is designed for audience simulation before a movie release and
specifically tailored for the Bengali film industry. The proposed approach addresses three
main concerns: through weak supervision, it cuts back on the need for manually labeled persona data, it gives way to persona-aware audience response generation by utilizing a coordinated multi-agent architecture, and it strengthens the reliability of the output through hybrid
neural-symbolic validation. In the framework, a three-stage pipeline is followed. To start
with, 6,114 Bengali movie reviews are turned into vectors using Language-Agnostic BERT
Sentence Embeddings (LaBSE) and grouped with K-means (K = 3) in order to discover three
main audience personas automatically without any manual annotation. In the second stage,
a model based on BanglaBERT is fine-tuned to classify personas, which is then utilized as a
neural validator, reaching an accuracy of 87.49% and an F1-score of 0.8733 under five-fold
cross-validation. The results of the experiments show that persona conformity was achieved
for all three personas with a mere single generation attempt on average, thus indicating high
efficiency and stability. The hybrid validation strategy was able to effectively deal with the
ambiguous persona cases, especially for the Enthusiastic Casual persona, where purely neural methods were not enough. This study presents a neuro-symbolic multi-agent audience
simulation framework for a low-resource language, a hybrid validation strategy for personaaware text generation, and a weakly supervised persona discovery pipeline, thus providing
practical value for data-driven decision-making in the Bengali film industry.
