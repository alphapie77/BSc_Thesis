Chapter 1
Introduction
The prediction of audience reactions has turned into an essential challenge in the contemporary film sector, especially concerning the decision-making based on releasing and marketing strategies. This study tackles the drawbacks of current systems that analyze sentiments
after the release by putting forward a framework for pre-release audience simulation that is
aware of the persona’s needs and is designed for Bengali cinema. This chapter describes the
research context and gives an overview of the suggested neuro-symbolic multi-agent system.
The organization of the chapter is as follows: Background, Motivation, Problem Statement,
Objectives, Research Challenges, and Thesis Organization.
1.1 Background
Over the past few years, the Bangladesh film industry has been heavily dependent on copying foreign cinema styles, plots, and storytellers’ skills rather than being creative, which has
probably been a downfall for the industry’s relationship with the audience and for the films
resonating with the audience. Consequently, numerous films that are made locally do not
meet the emotional, social, and cultural expectations of the viewers, being the Bangladeshi
audience. This escalating gap between filmmakers and the audience has caused a drop in audience engagement and uncertainty regarding the commercial and critical success of a film.
Hence, it has become a pressing need for the film industry to understand the Bangladeshi
audience’s perception and emotional response towards the local cinema for its sustainable
development. In the hunt for a solution to the aforementioned problem, the researchers here
relied on the assumption that the making of a culturally inclined, audience-perceived film
could only come through the analytical study of audience opinions. A dataset containing
